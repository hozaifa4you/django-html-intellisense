# Changelog

## 1.1.0

- Emmet works in Django templates out of the box (`div.card>p`); no `emmet.includeLanguages` setting needed.
- Suggestions open on their own inside attribute values, and accepting one replaces the word under the cursor, as in plain HTML files.
- If the Auto Close Tag extension also runs in Django templates (which inserts every closing tag twice), a warning offers to turn it off there in one click.

## 1.0.0

- Extension icon, and badges and links in the README.

## 0.1.0

- First release: VS Code's built-in HTML IntelliSense (tags, attributes, values, hover, auto-close, auto quotes) in `django-html` templates.
